Every rupee in and out, captured here; the earnings are in Insights

Inventory's job is to capture every purchase, count, expense and leak, and to keep kitchen costs in check. The earnings ladder (actual revenue → EBITDA) now lives only in Insights, so it isn't shown on two pages. The Overview opens on Kitchen cost control: food cost % against the 35% target, spend by group, unexplained loss, stock on hand and supplier dues. Books complete stays here, because this is where missing entries get added. The AI entry buttons are marked Coming soon.

About recipes and food cost: your idea, argued

You were right to keep recipes. On their own they only show what food should have cost. Counts show what it did cost. ZenOS uses both, and the difference is the most useful number a kitchen can watch.

Count-based food costRecipe-based food cost
AnswersHow much food did we really use?How much should each dish cost to make?
NeedsA count every 4th day plus purchase billsA recipe per dish (ingredients and quantities)
Works fromDay oneOnce the top 20 dishes have recipes. They usually make up most of sales
FeedsReal profit and EBITDA in Financial InsightsFood cost % per dish, price advice on the Menu page
TogetherActual food cost − recipe food cost = unexplained loss (over-portioning, waste not logged, theft)

A note on words: revenue means money coming in. What you mean by "actual revenue of the hotel" is closer to operating profit (EBITDA). The page shows both, labelled plainly.
